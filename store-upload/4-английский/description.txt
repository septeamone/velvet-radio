=== DESCRIPTION ===
Velvet Radio is a radio player for Chrome with every detail considered.

400+ stations from Russia and 28 countries. Sound up to 320 kbps. A refined matte interface in two themes: graphite and ivory. Tap a station and the music plays, even when the window is closed.

FEATURES
• Plays in the background: close the window and the music keeps going
• Countries with flags and genres for every country, search in English or Russian
• Song titles right in the list, a sound visualizer and a stream status indicator
• Quality choice for every station, up to 320 kbps
• If a stream drops, the extension switches to a backup and lowers the quality when needed
• Favorites, drag-and-drop ordering, keyboard shortcuts and media keys
• Russian and English interface

SETTINGS
• Data saver, wait before playback and resume on Chrome start
• Favorites sync through your Chrome account and backup
• The visualizer can be turned off: the player gets shorter and more stations fit on screen
• The classic interface stays available

PRIVACY
No analytics, ads or tracking. The extension sends nothing to the developer. All code is inside the extension.

Unofficial project. Station names and logos belong to their owners. Some stations broadcast from Russia and may be unavailable abroad.

=== SINGLE PURPOSE (Privacy section) ===
Playing internet radio: stations from Russia and other countries.

=== PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION (Privacy section) ===
offscreen: plays the radio in the background while the popup is closed (hidden audio document).
storage: favorites, station order, quality, theme, language and settings; optional sync through the Chrome account if the user turns it on.
Access to the stations' websites (host_permissions): only to show the current song title and to open HLS streams of those stations. The list is unchanged since version 1.0.0.
